Latest Food Trends & Culinary News

How African chefs and diaspora innovators are revolutionizing global fine dining and sustainable agriculture.

🌾 Superfoods • October 24, 2026

The Global Rediscovery of Fonio and Ancient Teff

Nutritionists worldwide are hailing Fonio, Africa’s oldest cultivated cereal, as an drought-resilient supergrain with a low glycemic index. In Dakar and Paris, pastry chefs are substituting conventional wheat with gluten-free Ethiopian Teff flour to achieve nutty, prebiotic-dense delicacies.

Tagged: Ancient Grains, Climate Resilient Crops

🏺 Fermentation • October 18, 2026

Fermentation Revival: Dawadawa and Iru in Modern Umami

Celebrated chefs across Lagos and London are celebrating traditional fermented locust beans (Iru/Dawadawa) as nature's ultimate umami seasoning. Packed with natural glutamates and digestive enzymes, this ancient ferment is transforming modern plant-forward gastronomy.

Tagged: Fermentation, Umami Flavor Science

🌶️ Spice Heritage • October 09, 2026

Artisanal Piri-Piri and Suya Blends Take Over Street Food

Smoky West African Suya pepper (Yaji)—blended with roasted peanut powder, ginger, chili, and ground alligator pepper—is commanding center stage in global urban street food festivals, inspiring cross-cultural barbecue collaborations from Johannesburg to New York.

Tagged: Suya Yaji, Diaspora Street Food
