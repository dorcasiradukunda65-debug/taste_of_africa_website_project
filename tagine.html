Moroccan Lamb Tagine with Apricots

An ancient Berber culinary tradition. Tender lamb shank braised in a conical clay vessel with sweet Turkish apricots, golden prunes, toasted almonds, honey, and the aromatic warmth of 27-spice Ras el Hanout.

Prep Time 25 Mins
Cook Time 1 hr 45 Mins
Origin Morocco / Maghreb
Flavor Sweet & Savory
Saved recipes persist across visits
Adjust Servings: 4 Servings

Ingredients List

Quantities scale dynamically with the buttons above.

  • Boneless lamb shoulder or shanks (cubed) 2 lbs
  • Dried golden apricots 1 cup
  • Pitted prunes ¾ cup
  • Ras el Hanout spice blend 1.5 tbsp
  • Ground cinnamon 1 tsp
  • Ground ginger & turmeric 1 tsp each
  • Saffron threads (steeped in warm water) ½ tsp
  • Wildflower honey 2 tbsp
  • Yellow onions (finely grated) 2 large
  • Blanched almonds (toasted) ½ cup
  • Extra virgin olive oil 3 tbsp
  • Fresh coriander & mint ½ cup

The Magic of the Earthen Tagine

The conical lid of the Moroccan Tagine traps rising condensation and returns moisture to the base, naturally braising meat in desert regions where water was traditionally precious. The hallmark blend of sweet dried fruits and savory spices reflects Andalusia and Berber intercultural synthesis.

Cooking Instructions

Follow these traditional methods for succulent, melt-in-the-mouth Moroccan braising.

Step 1: Marinate the Lamb in Saffron & Spices

In a large bowl, whisk together extra virgin olive oil, Ras el Hanout, ground ginger, turmeric, cinnamon, sea salt, black pepper, and the warm saffron water. Toss the cubed lamb shoulder in the spice paste until completely coated. Let rest for at least 30 minutes (or refrigerate overnight for depth).

Step 2: Sear the Lamb and Caramelize Aromatics

Heat a clay tagine or heavy enameled cast iron Dutch oven over medium heat. Sear the marinated lamb in batches for 5–7 minutes until lightly browned on all sides. Remove lamb, reduce heat to low, and add the grated onions and crushed garlic. Sauté gently until translucent and sweet.

Step 3: Slow Braise Under Conical Steam

Return the lamb and its resting juices to the pot. Add 1 cup of water or vegetable broth. Secure the conical lid tightly, reduce the flame to low, and braise undisturbed for 1 hour and 15 minutes, allowing steam to continually baste the meat.

Step 4: Glaze with Honey, Apricots & Prunes

In a separate small saucepan, simmer the dried apricots and prunes with 2 tablespoons of honey, a pinch of cinnamon, and ½ cup of braising liquid for 8 minutes until plump and glossy. Uncover the tagine, arrange the glistening fruits over the lamb, and simmer for an additional 15 minutes to thicken the sauce.

Step 5: Garnish and Communal Service

Scatter freshly toasted golden almonds and chopped coriander over the tagine. Serve steaming hot directly from the clay vessel with warm crusty Khobz bread or steamed couscous to soak up the amber gravy.
